I am a third-year PhD student in economics at Georgia State University. My research lies at the intersection of labor economics, public economics, and quantitative marketing.

My current work studies wage inequality and worker–firm sorting, how firms and consumers respond to tax and place-based policies, and product variety in consumer packaged goods markets.
