Publication 2022

The mechanism of household waste sorting behaviour: A study of Jiaxing, China

with Qiao Liu, Xin Shen, Bowei Chen, and Sonia S. Esfahani. International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 2022.

Using survey data from 541 urban and rural residents in Jiaxing, this paper integrates the theory of planned behavior with value-belief-norm theory to study household waste sorting. The results show that attitudes, subjective norms, perceived behavioral control, and perceived policy effectiveness help explain sorting intentions and behavior.

Publication 2022

Predicting sustainable food consumption across borders based on the theory of planned behavior: A meta-analytic structural equation model

with Xin Shen and Qiao Liu. PLOS ONE, 2022.

This paper uses meta-analytic structural equation modeling to compare original and extended theory-of-planned-behavior frameworks for sustainable food consumption. It synthesizes prior evidence to identify the main drivers of consumers' sustainable food purchasing decisions across contexts.

Publication 2023

The relationship between supply chain resilience, supply chain integration, and supply chain performance: A MASEM analysis

with Xin Shen, Qianhui Xu, Qiao Liu, and Markus Leibercht. Journal of Intelligent & Fuzzy Systems, 2023.

This study combines meta-analysis with structural equation modeling to examine how supply chain resilience relates to performance. Across 45 empirical studies, the paper finds that resilience improves performance and that supplier, internal, and customer integration partially mediate that link.
